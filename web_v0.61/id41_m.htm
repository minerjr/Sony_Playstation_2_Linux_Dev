Comments and suggestions
If you have any comments and suggestions, then please get in touch.  I need to know about all kinds of things relating to this web site, silly little typos, areas of knowledge I've brushed over too quickly, points I've laboured a bit too much... that kind of thing.

There are many ways to contact me, the best being through my project page on playstation2-linux.com, where there are areas to post bugs, ask for clarification of certain topics or ideas covered in the tutorials, and keep up to date with the latest projects.  Alternatively, you can e-mail me at eratosthenes@users.playstation2-linux.com.

Please also post feature requests, is there anything you'd like to know about the VUs, but can't find the relevant info yourself?  Ask, and I'll do my best to create a lesson out of it!

If you have written your own tutorial about the VUs, and would like it hosted, please send it to me and I'll include it on this web site.

I'd also love people to send me their completed VU demos, after the competition deadline has passed of course, and if you agree to it, I could dissect your demos and create further lessons and tutorials out of them.  I'm going to carry on VU coding after this competition has ended, because I'm an enthusiast.  And, you never know, there may be more competitions to enter in the future...

Until next update, happy microcoding!
